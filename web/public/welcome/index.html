What Verytas found

    What it checks

    Your brand book, read by something that never gets tired.

    Rules that read like your guidelines

    Write them the way your team already does. Banned words, tone, disclosures, channel by channel. Each finding quotes the rule it broke, so nobody argues with a black box.

    Claims meet your facts

    Numbers and promises are compared with the facts you keep, with their dates and sources.

    Secrets stay secret

    Code names and unreleased dates are caught before a creator posts them, even inside a screenshot.

    Logos, mascots and packaging

    Artwork is found in images and video frames, then checked for colour, stretch and crop.

    Faces with an agreement

    People in a post are matched to the ambassadors you approved, with their dates and channels.

    Conversations, with Inki on call

    Creators and reviewers talk next to the piece in question: files, replies, edits, meeting links. Mention @Inki and it answers from the record, only after you have said it may read along.

    How it works

    Three steps, and the middle one takes seconds.

    1. Teach it your brand

      Upload the brand book, facts sheet and artwork. Verytas drafts the rules; a person approves each one.

    2. Creators submit

      A caption, a carousel or a reel. Findings come back with the reason and a suggested fix.

    3. Your team decides

      Only what needs judgement reaches the queue. Every approval is signed and lands in an audit trail.

    Creators should hear yes faster. Brands should never find out from the comments. Verytas sits in between, so both get what they need before anything goes live.
